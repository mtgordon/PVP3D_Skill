**Why the targets cannot both be met within x0.2-x5 with one wall scale:** every lever moves Ba and Bp nearly together, so the
gap Bp - Ba barely changes (posterior x0.5 +1.1, x0.2 +2.9; walls x2 +2.5; apical x2 +1.2 mm). The base has Bp - Ba = -5.9 (P1) /
-6.4 (P2) mm; the paper needs Bp - Ba of at least about +4 (P1) / +9 (P2). The seam springs hold the two walls' sides together, so
they descend as one; only the PVW alone (+3.1 per halving) split them, which the shared wall scale excludes. The effects are also
not additive: stiff walls (x5) hold the posterior wall up and take away most of the posterior support's effect
(`L156_C1_parcus02_walls5_*`: P2 +4.0 / +2.7 against a linear estimate of ~+8 / +9).

## 4. Convergence notes

- Stiff walls converge fast (walls x5: 30-55 min, 0-1 failures); softer walls or a separately softened AVW / PVW crawl late in
  P1 / P2 (stopped: `L150_avwx05_P1`, `L150_pvwx2_P1`, `L154_wallsx05_*`).
- **Spurious states after many retries:** `L155_parcus02_P1` (45 retries) jumped 40-60 mm upward (the whole vagina) between t
  0.962 and 0.974 under rising load; `L150_parcusx2_P2` (72 retries) and `L150_apicalx05_healthy` (t 0.99 -> 1.0) show the same.
  Those late readings are unused; every result above was checked by its trace over the last 10 % of load. Lesson added to the
  skill's lessons-learned.
- All stops and reasons: `runs/_autostop.txt`.

## 5. Result against the stop rule

- **C3 (healthy posterior support x0.42, nothing else) meets both Bp targets on the full base** (P1 +5.0, 1.0 mm from +4; P2 +9.2,
  0.2 mm from +9) with the healthy model without prolapse (-6.1 / -12.9), **but Ba is 9.3 (P1) and 14.8 (P2) mm below the hymen**
  where the paper has it above.
- **C2 (posterior x0.42 + walls x5) keeps Ba at the hymen** (P1 -4.2, P2 +0.9) **but Bp is 10.1 mm short in both** (-6.1, -1.1).
- No set within x0.2-x5 with one shared wall scale does both: Ba is limited by Bp through the walls' coupling (section 2). What
  would split them (not tested, outside the user's choices so far): the PVW softer than the AVW (+3.1 mm of Bp - Ba per halving of
  the PVW alone), weaker or fewer seam springs between the walls' sides, or an anterior support that lifts Ba without the PVW.
- Wall times: C3 full base 0:43 (healthy) / 0:47 (P1) / 1:01 (P2); C2 full base 0:30 / 0:50 / 1:29 (8 / 6 threads).
