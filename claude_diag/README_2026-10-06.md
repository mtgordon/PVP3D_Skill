Every run is `claude_diag/runs/<run>/<run>.feb`. Ba / Bp / C in mm at t = 1, + below the hymen (POP-Q as Bump 1996,
tools/pop_q_bump.py; fixed hymenal plane PM_Plane at rest, as Luo et al.'s own reference plane: their Figure 1 caption, "the
approximate position of the hymen at rest"). **Luo et al. define their Ba / Bp as the points 3 cm above the hymen (= Bump's

(P2's Ba is our inference). The real constraint is the gap **Bp - Ba: P1 needs about +4 or more, P2 about +9 or more**.
Every end state was traced over t 0.9 / 0.95 / 1 and checked for jumps over the last 30 states (tools/last_moves.py);
every run is in run_log_notes.csv.

## 6. Against the stop rule and recommendation

- **Band line (the default since 10-07):** the 7.5 mm stiff seam band on the AVW + cervix (59 % of the AVW), PVW band-free,
  apical x6.5, posterior x0.35. Healthy no prolapse; **P1 -0.8 / +3.1 (meets)**; **P2 Bp +10.2 (meets), Ba +7.5 (misses)**.
  The 9 mm band (72 %) does P1 slightly better (+4.1) but stiffens most of the AVW. P2's Ba is the one target no set meets:
  P2's apical x0.4 drops the AVW 9-10 mm more than P1 in every set; the best P2 gap is +3.6 against ~+9 needed (and the
  paper states Ba above the hymen only for its 30 % apical runs).
- **What opens Bp - Ba (band line):** posterior support (floor x0.35), no band on the PVW, a wide AND stiff band on the AVW.
  Apical sets the height only (x6 / x7 / x8: gap +2.2 / +2.1 / +2.3). Para, PM_conn, sphincter side: nothing.
- **Tube line (the default since 10-07):** the closed apex stops the apical inversion, so the curves can carry pressure.
  The tube's walls still move together (posterior x0.4, a softer PVW-side curve: both walls drop), so the tube cannot yet
  give Bp below Ba; Bp is ~14 mm short in P1. A fully elastic curve-PVW join (L204, penalty 0.05) opens the gap only ~1 mm (-4.0 -> -2.9): the tube's walls still move
  together. The band line's contact-only seam lets the PVW edge SLIDE past the AVW; an elastic join does not.
- **Decisions for the user:** accept P2's Ba miss for the band default, or name a lever for it; whether the 9 mm band is
  acceptable; the next tube step after L203.
